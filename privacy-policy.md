# Privacy Policy for Dino Dash 3D: Canyon Runner

    Last updated: November 24, 2026

    This Privacy Policy explains how "Dino Dash 3D: Canyon Runner" ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Raiyan Hasan,
    handles information when you use our mobile application.

    ### 1. Information We Collect
    Dino Dash 3D is designed as a casual offline endless runner game.

    * **Personal Information**: We do NOT collect, transmit, or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your
    name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone data.
    * **Local Game Data**: The App stores gameplay progress (such as high scores, collected egg currency, audio preferences, and
    unlocked dinosaurs) locally on your device storage (via local device preferences). This data is never sent to external servers
    without your permission.
    * **Anonymous Technical Data**: The game is built using the Unity engine. Unity may automatically collect non-personally
    identifiable diagnostic and hardware information (such as device model, OS version, resolution, and crash logs) strictly to
    ensure game stability and performance.

    ### 2. Third-Party Services
    The App may utilize third-party SDKs provided by:
    * **Unity Technologies**: For game engine functionality, rendering, and performance diagnostics. Please review Unity's privacy
    policy at: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
    * **Google Play Services**: For standard app delivery, core operating system compatibility, and optional
    achievements/leaderboards.

    ### 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR Compliance)
    Our game does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the
    European Union). Since the game does not collect personal data, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy
    Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

    ### 4. Permissions
    Dino Dash 3D requests only minimal, non-sensitive device permissions necessary for standard gameplay:
    * `VIBRATE`: To provide subtle haptic feedback when swiping, jumping, or collecting items.
    * `INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: For standard Google Play core license verification and store integration.

    ### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
    We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
